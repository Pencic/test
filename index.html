What is NAS & TrueNAS?

A NAS (Network-Attached Storage) is like your own personal or business cloud server. It stores all your files, photos, and videos in one place and makes them accessible from any device on your network or securely over the internet.

TrueNAS is an open-source, enterprise-grade operating system for NAS servers. It uses the powerful ZFS filesystem, which protects your data with advanced backup, snapshots, and self-healing technology. With TrueNAS, you get the flexibility of custom-built hardware and the reliability of professional storage — without being locked into big tech ecosystems.

TrueNAS vs Synology/QNAP

Feature TrueNAS (Our Builds) Synology / QNAP
Cost No license fees, open-source Proprietary software, extra licenses for features
Flexibility Custom hardware, fully upgradeable Fixed hardware, limited upgrades
Data Protection ZFS filesystem with snapshots, bitrot protection Standard RAID, less robust
Performance Enterprise-grade, scalable Consumer-focused, limited under heavy loads
Ownership You own everything, no lock-in Tied to brand ecosystem
